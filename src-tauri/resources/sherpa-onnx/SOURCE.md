The bundled Windows x64 native files are from sherpa-onnx v1.13.8:
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/download/v1.13.8/sherpa-onnx-v1.13.8-win-x64-shared-MD-Release-no-tts.tar.bz2

They include ONNX Runtime v1.28.2. The relevant license texts are shipped in
SHERPA_LICENSE.txt and ONNXRUNTIME_LICENSE.txt. InterviewCue downloads model
weights separately from the official sherpa-onnx asr-models release; weights
are not included in the installer.
